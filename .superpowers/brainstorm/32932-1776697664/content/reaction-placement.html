A: Below Bubble + Smiley in Action Bar

Reaction pills sit below the message bubble. A 😊 button in the existing hover action bar opens the quick-pick row. The "+" on the reaction row opens the full picker.

Slack / Discord style. Reactions are always visible. Action bar gets a smiley icon alongside edit/delete/reply.
